    How the blocks are chosen

    • Focus time runs 8 am to 10 pm, leaving a wind-down hour before 11 pm.
    • 15-minute buffer before and after every class.
    • Lunch 12-1 and dinner 6-7 stay free.
    • Each block is 1 to 3 cycles of 50 minutes on, 10 off, matching the timer.
    • Homework is capped at 4 hours on weekdays and 5 hours on weekends, using the longest blocks first, so the week stays doable.
    • Teal blocks are protected time for internship applications and planning your future. They sit early in the week (Mon to Thu) so Friday is for finishing, not starting.
